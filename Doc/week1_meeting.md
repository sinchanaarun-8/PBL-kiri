## Discussion

* The team discussed the basic concept and purpose of Inter-Process Communication.
* Since the project is still in the initial research stage, the team decided to study IPC from the fundamentals before making implementation decisions.
* The research was divided into four areas so that each member can work independently without unnecessary duplication.
* Each member will first understand the general concept and then study the topic specifically in the context of operating-system-based IPC.
* The team decided to maintain the research in separate Markdown files inside the common GitHub repository.
* Members will work only on their assigned research files and push their completed work to the repository.
* Reliable technical and academic sources will be used, and references will be included in each research document.
* The team will review all four research files together before deciding the final technical direction of the project.
